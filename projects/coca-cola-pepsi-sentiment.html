Coca-Cola & Pepsi: Stock Price and Public Sentiment

A project combining social-media sentiment and stock-price data to explore changes in public sentiment alongside market movements.

At a glance

Public opinion alongside market movements

My work focused on collecting and preparing sentiment data and making it explorable alongside stock prices. The analysis does not establish that sentiment predicts returns.

Research · Python · Visualization

My contribution

Social-media sentiment collection and preparation, Python sentiment scripts, data cleaning, and Streamlit dashboards visualizing sentiment alongside stock-price trends.

Portfolio role

This team project developed my Python and visualization skills. Stock prediction and the final report were primarily handled by a teammate; this case study focuses on my sentiment and dashboard work.
