Coca-Cola & Pepsi: Stock Price and Public Sentiment

Exploring changes in social-media sentiment alongside Coca-Cola and Pepsi stock-price movements.

Python · Sentiment analysis · Streamlit

At a glance

My contribution focused on sentiment data preparation and Streamlit visualizations, making public-opinion patterns easier to explore alongside stock-price trends.

The question

How do public-sentiment patterns change over time alongside stock-price movements?

My contribution

Collected and prepared social-media sentiment data, wrote Python sentiment scripts, cleaned the data, and built Streamlit dashboards comparing sentiment with stock-price trends.

Team scope and interpretation

Stock prediction and the final report were primarily handled by a teammate. This case study covers my sentiment and dashboard work. Plotting the series together does not establish that sentiment predicts returns.
