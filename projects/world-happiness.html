World Happiness Factors

An early statistics project exploring relationships between national happiness scores and social and economic factors.

R · Correlation · Regression

At a glance

This project built my foundation in correlation, regression, and explaining statistical results with visual evidence.

The question

How do GDP, social support, healthy life expectancy, and generosity relate to national happiness scores?

Methods

Pearson correlation, multiple linear regression, scatterplots, and regional comparison in R.

Project context

This is one of my earlier data projects. It represents foundational statistical analysis rather than a current predictive product. The observed relationships should not be interpreted as causal effects.
