Higher Education Outcomes Analysis

An ongoing College Scorecard capstone examining completion, cost, financial aid, and earnings across U.S. institutions. The work combines missing-data assessment, regression, Lasso, and PCA to understand both the observed patterns and which institutions the analysis represents.

Question

How do institutional characteristics relate to completion and earnings, and how does missing information affect the institutions included in the analysis?

What I did

Prepared institution-level data from a source with 6,273 institutions, reviewed missingness, estimated completion and earnings regressions, compared completion samples with and without admission rate, applied 10-fold cross-validated Lasso, and performed standardized PCA on four numeric predictors.

Methods

R · EDA · Complete-case analysis · Multiple linear regression · Lasso · Principal component analysis

Preliminary findings

Cost of attendance was positively associated with completion and earnings; Pell Grant participation was negatively associated with both, conditional on the other included predictors. These institution-level associations do not establish causal effects.

Missing data change who is represented

Admission rate was missing for 69.6% of institutions in the source. Excluding it increased the usable completion-model sample from 1,618 to 2,138. Adjusted R² was 0.4815 with admission rate and 0.4082 without it. Because both the predictor set and the sample changed, this comparison does not isolate the contribution of admission rate.

What PCA adds

Across 1,727 complete observations, PC1 contrasted higher cost with higher admission rates and Pell participation. PC2 was dominated by undergraduate enrollment. Institution types overlap in the plot; the first two components capture 70.3% of standardized predictor variance, with 29.7% outside this view.

Model scope and next steps

The earnings regression used 1,949 institutions. Reported regression R² values describe in-sample fit, not held-out predictive performance. Lasso retained all predictor terms at the selected minimum-error penalty. Missing data limit generalization to the full source dataset. Next steps include comparing Random Forest with linear regression and Lasso on a common sample and evaluation procedure, then developing the dashboard.

Data and analytical choices

The College Scorecard source contains 6,273 institution records. Complete observations vary by analysis: 1,618 for the completion model, 1,949 for earnings, and 1,727 for PCA. Suppressed or non-numeric entries were treated as missing. The models use institution-level records; they do not describe an individual student's expected outcome.

Why it matters for decisions

Comparisons built only on complete records can leave out large parts of the original population. Before using an analysis to compare institutions or allocate further research, I would examine sample coverage alongside fit and test performance on held-out data.
