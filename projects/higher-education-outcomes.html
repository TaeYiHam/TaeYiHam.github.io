My contribution

Prepared institution-level College Scorecard data, assessed missingness, estimated completion and earnings regressions, compared samples with and without admission rate, applied 10-fold cross-validated Lasso, and performed standardized PCA. Extended the project with a Python-to-SQLite audit and an Excel report for sample-count checks, institution-type summaries, and missingness review.

SQL validation & Excel reporting

The extension audits the same 14 selected fields used in the R analysis. A Python importer converts finite numeric values to numbers, maps suppressed or non-numeric entries to NULL, and derives institution-type labels. SQLite checks identifiers, defines complete-case membership for four analyses, and exports counts, row-level membership flags, missingness, and institution-type summaries.

What the Excel report contains

A Python-generated workbook organizes four worksheets: Validation, Definitions, Summary, and Missingness. Difference and IF formulas compare SQL counts with reported R counts. Institution-type summaries include the available-observation counts beside the means.

To update this saved report, rerun the audit and regenerate the workbook from its exported results.

Findings and evidence

Cost of attendance was positively associated with completion and earnings; Pell Grant participation was negatively associated with both, conditional on the other included predictors. Completion R² was 0.4834 and earnings R² was 0.5053. These describe in-sample fit, not held-out prediction.

Adjusted completion R² was 0.4815 with admission rate and 0.4082 without it. Lasso retained all predictor terms at the minimum-error penalty. In PCA, PC1 contrasted cost with admission rates and Pell participation; PC2 was dominated by enrollment. Institution types overlap in the plot.

The question

How do cost, financial aid, selectivity, enrollment, and institution type relate to college completion and earnings?

Data and sample

The audited source snapshot contains 6,273 institution records. Suppressed and non-numeric entries were treated as missing. Complete-case counts vary: 1,618 for the completion model, 2,138 for the model excluding admission rate, 1,949 for earnings, and 1,727 for PCA. The saved SQL missingness output reports admission rate missing for 69.55% of the source.

Interpretation and next steps

These are institution-level associations, not causal effects or estimates for individual students. Missing data limit generalization. The next validation step is to independently export R sample-membership flags and reconcile them with the SQL output, then review the workbook in desktop Excel. Random Forest comparison on a common sample and evaluation procedure remains planned.

Statistical results are documented in Weekly Updates #5 and #6. The SQL and Excel extension was reviewed on October 23, 2026. The project and source repository remain private; selected methods and aggregate results are summarized here.

Analysis workflow

View the three steps
  1. Analyze in R Clean selected fields and examine completion and earnings with regression, Lasso, and PCA.
  2. Audit in SQL Use Python to load selected fields into SQLite, check identifiers, and export sample-membership flags and summaries.
  3. Report in Excel Compare SQL counts with reported R references using formulas, and organize definitions, summaries, and missingness.
